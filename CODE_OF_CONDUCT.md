# Code of Conduct

Orbi follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient, and assume good intent. Welcome people of every
background and level of experience. Disagree about ideas, never about people.
Harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing someone's
private information are not acceptable here, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, contact the maintainer privately through
[mobapptech.com/#contact](https://www.mobapptech.com/#contact). Every report
is read, answered and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments,
commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who
do so repeatedly or seriously, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
